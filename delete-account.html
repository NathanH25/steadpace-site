Delete your SteadPace account

App: SteadPace (com.steadpace.app)

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open SteadPace and go to the Household tab.
  2. Tap the People section.
  3. Tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you can't open the app, email steadpace1@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my SteadPace account", your display name and your household's name. Because accounts are anonymous, we may ask you to confirm with a household member. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account
  • Your classmate-directory listing and roommate requests
  • Your display name and profile
  • Your household memberships (if you were the last member, the household and its data are deleted too)
  • Messages you sent and your shares of shared expenses
  • House info notes you wrote (door codes, Wi-Fi, contacts)
  • People you blocked

What is kept

  • Expenses you paid for, and grocery, chore and meal items you added, stay in your former household's shared history, labeled "Former member", so its records still make sense to the people who remain.
  • Reports about messages are kept for up to 90 days for safety review, then deleted.
  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security.

Data on your phone

Your personal planner (budget, classes, reminders, shifts) is stored only on your phone and is not part of your account. Remove it with Me → Your data & privacy → Erase all data, or by uninstalling SteadPace.
