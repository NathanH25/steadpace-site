Shared expenses, pay and estimates

SteadPace helps you track who paid for what. It does not move money, process payments or guarantee that anyone will pay you. Balances are only as accurate as what members enter. SteadPace is not a bank, lender or debt collector. Balances are informal records to help you settle up, not bills, invoices or legally binding debts. A shared monthly budget is a planning number only.

Hours and earnings in Work & pay are your own estimates before taxes and deductions. They are not payroll or timekeeping records; your employer's records and pay stubs control.

House info notes are shared with everyone in your household and are not end-to-end encrypted. Don't store bank, email or other account passwords there.
